CRT CURRICULUM

Campus Recruitment Training Modules

Designed for colleges, universities, and individual candidates preparing for competitive IT and non-IT placement drives.

Quantitative Aptitude

Numbers, percentages, profit & loss, time & work, permutations & combinations, probability, data interpretation, and high-speed mental math shortcuts.

Logical Reasoning

Deductive logic, syllogisms, blood relations, seating arrangements, coding-decoding, series completion, and analytical puzzles.

Verbal Ability & Soft Skills

Grammar essentials, vocabulary enhancement, reading comprehension, sentence correction, corporate email etiquette, and group discussion tactics.

Network Administration Track

CCNA routing, switching, VLANs, NAT, ACLs, DHCP, DNS, IP subnetting, network troubleshooting, and server management.

Technical Problem Solving

Data structures, algorithm complexity, C/Python/Java programming logic, and code optimization for technical assessment rounds.

One-on-One Mock Interviews

Simulated HR and technical interview panels with instant structured feedback, body language coaching, and confidence building.
